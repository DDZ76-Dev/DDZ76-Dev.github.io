iOS 6 Tube Revival

A personal, non-commercial project that keeps the built-in video app on an old iPhone (iOS 6) working.

The original video app that shipped with iOS 6 can no longer sign in, because the sign-in method it used was retired years ago. This project adds a modern, secure sign-in using Google's "sign in on another device" method: the iPhone shows a short code, and you approve it at google.com/device on any other phone or computer. Your Google password is never typed into, sent to, or stored on the old iPhone.

What it does after you sign in
  • Shows your YouTube subscriptions and the latest videos from those channels
  • Shows and edits your playlists
  • Lets you like or dislike videos
  • Shows your own uploads

It uses the YouTube Data API and runs entirely on the user's own iPhone. There is no server, no website account, and no data collection. See the privacy policy for details.
