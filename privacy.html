Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

iOS 6 Tube Revival ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial add-on for the built-in video app on an iOS 6 iPhone. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and who it is shared with. In short: everything stays on your own iPhone, and the developer never receives any of your data.

Information the app accesses

When you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks Google for permission to use the YouTube Data API on your behalf (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). With that permission it reads and changes only what you ask it to in the video app:

  • your YouTube channel's name and ID
  • your subscriptions, and the public videos of the channels you subscribe to
  • your playlists and the videos in them (including adding, removing, creating, renaming, or deleting playlists when you do so)
  • your likes and dislikes (including liking or disliking a video when you tap the button)
  • your own uploaded videos

The app does not access your Google password, email, contacts, location, or any other Google service. You sign in on Google's own page on another device; your password is never entered on, sent to, or stored on the iPhone.

How the information is used

The information is used only to show your YouTube lists inside the video app on your iPhone and to carry out the actions you take there (for example, subscribing or adding a video to a playlist). It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Where it is stored

  • The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored only on your iPhone, in a file readable only by the video app.
  • Lists fetched from YouTube are kept in the app's memory for a few minutes while it is open, so returning to a list is quick, and are not sent anywhere else.
  • Your "For You" list (public video IDs only) and a list of which public videos are YouTube Shorts are saved on the iPhone, in a file readable only by the video app, so they show quickly next time.
  • There is no server, database, or cloud storage run by the developer. The developer never receives your tokens or YouTube data.

Sharing

Your Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone. The app talks to:

  • Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com), for sign-in and the YouTube Data API.
  • Public video services that the video app already uses to look up and play public videos: a TubeRepair server (which provides video listings and playback for old devices) and the Return YouTube Dislike API (public like and dislike estimates). These receive only public video IDs or search words, the same as when you browse without signing in. They never receive your sign-in tokens, account details, or any information identifying you or your account.
  • youtube.com, to leave Shorts out of your "For You" list: for short videos the app checks whether the public address youtube.com/shorts/<video ID> exists. Each check sends only one public video ID, without your sign-in tokens or any cookies.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using its sign-in features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

  • Sign out in the video app deletes the stored tokens from the iPhone immediately.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google Account › Security › Third-party apps & services). Revoking access stops the app from reaching your data; tokens left on the iPhone stop working.
  • Removing the add-on from the iPhone deletes the app and its stored data.

Security

All connections to Google use HTTPS. Tokens are stored in a file accessible only to the video app on the iPhone.

Children

The app is a personal project and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes
